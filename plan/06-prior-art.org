Reusable: byte/stream idioms, =proxy= shapes for =TextReader= / =TextWriter=, the rendezvous channel, the delegate patterns. Not reusable as-is: it is a divergent copy, not conditionals; based on pre-1.3 threading (before =nrepl.util.threading=, =SessionThread=, JVMTI, the print rework). Its interrupt is the cooperative-only design this plan rejects (D3): no forced stop existed in its .NET target at the time.
