* Cross-cutting concerns
** Bytes and streams
- Java =byte[]= is signed, CLR =byte-array= is =System.Byte[]= (0–255). ~src/clojure/nrepl/bencode.clj:97~'s =unchecked-byte= narrowing is the identity on the CLR (=B3=). Fixture literals at ~test/clojure/nrepl/bencode_test.clj:186-192~ are negative and wrap silently to 255 in a seq literal: they need =(bit-and x 0xFF)=.
- EOF: =InputStream.read(byte[],off,len)= returns -1, =Stream.Read= returns 0; the loop test is =(and (zero? n) (pos? len))= because an empty netstring =0:= is a legal zero-length read (=L1=).
- =clojure.lang.PushbackInputStream= exists on the CLR and is used over a =BufferedStream= (=E4=); =PushbackTextReader= / =LineNumberingTextReader= on the text side.
- The 1-arg =(.Write s buf)= compiles against =ReadOnlySpan= and throws at runtime (=E2=): every write is 3-arg or =WriteByte=; 9 sites in =bencode.clj=.
- UTF-8: 10 sites, 2 helper fns; =(String. bytes "UTF-8")= compiles and throws at runtime (=E3=).
- =extend-protocol BencodeSerializable java.lang.Number= (~bencode.clj:366~): the CLR cannot =extend-protocol= on =Int32= / =Byte= (=B13=) → an =integer?= branch in the =Object= fallback under =#?(:cljr …)=.
- =clojure.clr.io=: =text-reader= / =text-writer= (also over =Socket= and =Stream=); no =file=, =reader=, =writer=, =resource= (=B15=, =L3=); =edn/read= needs a =PushbackTextReader=.
- =(RT/classForName "[B")= returns nil silently on the CLR; the =<bytes= dispatch needs =|System.Byte[]|= (=L5=).
** Java static helper classes and missing imports (missed by the first audit)
- Only =System.*= is auto-imported on ClojureCLR. =Integer/parseInt= (×4: =misc= ×2, =version=, =cmdline=), =Long/MAX_VALUE= (×3: =transport=, =core=, =cmdline=), =Character/isUpperCase= (×2: =completion=), =Byte/TYPE= + =Byte/toUnsignedInt= (=bencode=), =Class/forName= (=cmdline=), =Arrays/sort= (=bencode=), =Semaphore= (=transport= tty), =Iterable= (=interruptible_eval=, =completion=) are compile errors; =Thread/currentThread= (×14) and =Thread/sleep= (×30, mostly tests) need =(:import [System.Threading Thread])= or the seam. =Math/…=, =String/valueOf= compile and fail at runtime (=L2=, =L3=).
- =try= / =catch Exception= around JVM-only interop gives no protection: the unresolved symbol fails the whole top-level form at compile time (=L2=).
- Member names do not alias: =.exists= → =.Exists=, =.getParentFile= → =.Directory=; lookup is case-sensitive; a =def= evaluated at load turns such a runtime miss into a load failure (=config.clj=, =L3=).
** Exceptions and names
- =Throwable= does not resolve: 15 sites in shared files (~misc.clj:17,28~, ~transport.clj:65,73,115~, ~server.clj:28,69~, ~ack.clj:49~, ~interruptible_eval.clj:134,159,161,169~) plus JVM-only files.
- =.getMessage= → =ex-message= (24 hits, PR 1). =safe-handle= (~src/clojure/nrepl/transport.clj:261-283~) currently throws =MissingMethodException= on the CLR error path and the client receives nothing (=L8=).
- =java.net.SocketException(String)= cannot be constructed on .NET; the disconnection exception goes through =nrepl.socket= (PR 3).
- Map: =EOFException→EndOfStreamException=, =IllegalArgumentException→ArgumentException=, =IllegalStateException→InvalidOperationException=, =InterruptedException→ThreadInterruptedException=, =ClosedChannelException→SocketException(Interrupted)=, =URISyntaxException→UriFormatException=, =ThreadDeath→ThreadAbortException / OperationCanceledException= (=TH6=), =Compiler$CompilerException→Compiler+CompilerException=.
- Wire-visible =:ex= / =:root-ex=: =(str (class e))= is ="class java.lang.X"= on the JVM and ="System.X"= on the CLR with the same expression (=B6=, D6). Tests at ~test/clojure/nrepl/core_test.clj:496-497,844-938~ match on these; =(/ 1 0)= throws =System.ArithmeticException= (=B1=).
- Uncatchable on .NET: =StackOverflowException=, =AccessViolationException= (R13).
- =format= uses Java-style specifiers on ClojureCLR (=B4=); =(str true)= is ="True"= and =(str 1.0)= is ="1"= (=L8=): =sanitize-meta= coerces booleans explicitly (PR 1).
- Unmatched optional regex groups are ="" = on the CLR, =nil= on the JVM (=L7=); =var-symbol?= and =split-by-leading-literals= in =completion= need =not-empty=.
** Threads, interrupt, executors
- =Thread.interrupt= on the JVM is a sticky, pollable flag; =Thread.Interrupt= on .NET has no pollable flag, is latched until the next wait (=TH2=, =TH3=), wakes every =WaitSleepJoin= wait within ~1 ms (=TH1=) and never unblocks a socket read (=TH4=). Sites: ~src/clojure/nrepl/util/threading.clj:86~, ~transport.clj:59,62,67~, ~middleware/session.clj:223~, ~test/clojure/nrepl/core_test.clj:507~.
- Forced stop: =Thread.Abort= is gone (=TH5=); =ControlledExecution.Run= wraps the body and aborts it 16–18 ms after =Cancel= (=TH6=), with the limits in =TH7=. ~util/threading.clj:62-91~, ~util/jvmti.clj~, =JvmtiAgent=, ~core_test.clj:452-546~ map onto it (D3).
- Executors: =listen=, =transport=, =handle=, =thread-reaper=, =ephemeral= are cached pools from =DaemonThreadFactory=. The infinite loops (accept ~server.clj:234~, per-connection handle ~server.clj:80~, reader ~transport.clj:58~) and the ephemeral user evals get dedicated threads on the CLR (D1, D2); the reaper is a dedicated thread, not a =Timer= (=TH11=).
- =run-with= does not convey bindings (~src/clojure/nrepl/cmdline.clj:153~); the session pushes bindings explicitly; ClojureCLR var frames are =[ThreadStatic]= (=B18=).
- =SynchronousQueue= has no .NET equivalent; a =Monitor=-based one-slot handoff keeps the semantics (=C7=, R12).
- =BlockingQueue.poll(long, TimeUnit)= → =TryTake(out T, int)= with the =by-ref= and Int32 rules of =C8=; =Long/MAX_VALUE= (~core.clj:17~, ~transport.clj:47~) → =infinite-ms=.
- Clojure fns are not delegates: =(Thread. f)=, =(reify Runnable)=, =.submit= need =gen-delegate= (=C4=); =^Runnable= / =.run= in ~session.clj:153-160,206-208~ stay under =#?(:clj …)=.
- =Thread$State/TERMINATED= → =.IsAlive= (=TH12=); daemon → =IsBackground=true=; an uncaught exception on any thread kills the process (=TH13=).
** Classloaders
- =DynamicClassLoader=, context classloader, =Compiler/LOADER= (~src/clojure/nrepl/misc.clj:92-98~, ~middleware/session.clj:128-131,212~, ~util/classloader.clj~, =DaemonThreadFactory=, =SessionThread=, 4 tests in ~core_test.clj:946-1026~) have no CLR analog; all =:clj=-only; the =nrepl.util.classloader= require leaves =session.clj= and =util/threading.clj=.
- =Compiler/eval= is 1-arg on the CLR (=A6=); ~load_file.clj:26-29~ binds three =internal= vars reachable by reflection (=A7=); =SOURCE= / =SOURCE_PATH= are =*source-path*= / =*file*=.
** Sockets, TLS, URIs
- The JVM encodes TLS and Unix-ness in socket subtypes (~src/clojure/nrepl/socket.clj:100,106,119-132~); .NET has one =Socket= class and layers =SslStream=. The CLR sibling =nrepl/socket.cljr= implements the contract table in 03-design.
- The =java.nio= channel/stream split and the JDK-4509080 workaround (~socket.clj:145-189~) are JVM-only; the =Writable= protocol stays because the NIO path is not an =OutputStream=.
- Semantics: =ServerSocket.bind= implies listen, .NET needs =Listen=; =isConnected= is sticky-true, =Socket.Connected= flips after a failed I/O (=F6=); =--bind localhost= resolves to =::1= first on .NET (=F3=, =B16=); =NetworkStream= failures arrive as =IOException= wrapping =SocketException= (~server.clj:87~ and four test assertions); never set =ReuseAddress= (=F5=).
- TLS: JSSE → =SslStream= with =CreateFromPem= over =AsSpan=, =CopyWithPrivateKey=, =CustomRootTrust= (=F7=); PEM scanning (~src/clojure/nrepl/tls.clj:84-149~) is pure. TLS tests already skipped on Windows.
- =java.net.URI= (~src/clojure/nrepl/cmdline.clj:636~, ~socket.clj:96-111~, ~core.clj:189-239~): =System.Uri= adds a trailing slash to the host form (=F8=) → =as-nrepl-uri-string= by hand, =parse-uri= for =url-connect=.
** Writers and output capture
- JVM =*out*= chain: =PrintWriter= → =OutputStreamWriter= (platform default charset: latent mojibake at ~src/clojure/nrepl/middleware/print.clj:67~, ~util/out.clj:94-95~, fixed in PR 1b) → =CallbackBufferedOutputStream= (UTF-8). CLR: one =TextWriter= proxy; buffer semantics move from bytes to UTF-16 units.
- =with-open= calls =.close= on the JVM, =.Dispose= on the CLR (=B7=); ~print.clj:81~ relies on close → flush → callback ordering, which a proxy =Dispose(bool)= preserves (=E10=).
- =System/setOut= and =bindRoot= of =*out*= are process-global and never undone; =unwrap-standard-streams= is added (PR 5b). ClojureCLR's root =*out*= bypasses =Console.Out= and does not auto-flush (=E7=).
- Line separators: =System/lineSeparator= → =Environment/NewLine= (=B12=).
** Reflection, completion, lookup
- No classpath. CLR sources: loaded assemblies (=AppDomain.CurrentDomain.GetAssemblies()=, guard =ReflectionTypeLoadException=), =CLOJURE_LOAD_PATH= dirs; the =with-classpath-cache= key must change (the classpath hash is a constant on the CLR, =L7=).
- Object model: =Class→Type=, =getName→FullName= / =(str type)= (nested =A+B=, generics =List`1=), =getPackage→Namespace=, plus =GetProperties= / =GetEvents= (=get_X= / =set_X= accessors leak as =:method= unless filtered by =IsSpecialName=). New candidate kinds =:property=, =:event= (D8).
- The ~53 Java =String= member calls in ~src/clojure/nrepl/util/completion.clj~ (31 on live paths) do not exist on the CLR and fail at runtime; 12 =.length= / =.charAt= sites have no =clojure.string= equivalent (=L7=). ClojureCLR's own =clojure.string= prefix/suffix/search functions are culture-sensitive (=B9=): PR 2's helper block gets ordinal CLR branches.
- =Keyword.table= (JVM) vs =Keyword._symKeyMap= (CLR, =B11=). =ns-resolve= returns nil for unknown dotted symbols on both hosts (=B10=).
- =io/resource= consumers: ~src/clojure/nrepl/version.clj~ and ~misc.clj:144-155~; var =:file= is load-path-relative on both hosts and =RT/FindFile= resolves it (=L8=).
** Process, env, config
- =System/exit= (4 sites), =shutdown-agents=, =(.join (Thread/currentThread))=, =deleteOnExit=, =sun.misc.Signal= live in =cmdline.clj= and go through the runtime seam from the CLR entry (=C11=).
- =System/getProperty= is a compile error on the CLR: =java.version=, =java.specification.version=, =jdk.attach.allowAttachSelf=, =nrepl.version=, =user.home=, test =nrepl.basedir=. =misc/java-version= must be nil on the CLR (not 8), and its two consumers are in seams (=L2=).
- ~src/clojure/nrepl/config.clj~ is filesystem/env only; =io/file= is 6 of its 16 sites; =Environment.GetFolderPath(UserProfile)= returns the home even with =HOME= unset (=L3=).
- =describe= reports =:versions {:nrepl :clojure :java}= (~src/clojure/nrepl/middleware.clj:61-68~); the CLR adds =:clr= and both hosts add =:aux :host= (D13). =nrepl.version= is ="" = from a source checkout on both hosts.
** Tests
- =nubank/matcher-combinators=: 198 =is+= assertions in 12 files, 7 namespaces require =matcher-combinators.matchers= directly, 12 distinct matcher shapes (D9). ~test/clojure/nrepl/test_helpers.clj~ uses =com.hypirion.io Pipe=, =Runtime/exec=, =java.net.ServerSocket=, =future-cancel= (which blocks on a spinning body on the CLR, =TH9=); =kaocha=, =locksmith= are JVM-only; =test.check= needs =clr.test.check=.
- ~core_test.clj~ (1068 LOC) encodes JVM interrupt/classloader/exception-text behaviour and must load first on the CLR (seven test namespaces require it); the per-line gating table is in slice 9.
- Coverage gaps closed by the roadmap's new tests: thread naming, rendezvous backpressure, reader-loop termination, the forced-stop guarantees, surrogate handling, =unwrap-standard-streams=, =safe-handle=.
